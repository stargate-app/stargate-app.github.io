What is Stargate

What Stargate is

A cross-chain bridge on LayerZero

Stargate is a cross-chain bridge built on LayerZero, a messaging protocol that passes instructions between blockchains. It moves stablecoins and ETH from one chain to another, so funds you hold on one network can be used on a different one.

Unified liquidity pools

Instead of minting a wrapped copy of your token, the bridge keeps pools of the asset on each chain. You deposit into the pool on the source chain and receive the asset from the pool on the destination chain.

Pools vs wrapped

Pool bridge vs wrapped-token bridge

Bridges move value in two common ways. The method decides what you receive and where the risk sits.

In practice

With a pool bridge you usually end up with the asset you recognise on the other chain. A large transfer can still be limited by how much liquidity the destination pool holds.

How to bridge

How to bridge with Stargate

Quote first

The interface shows what you will receive on the destination chain, the bridge fee and the estimated gas before anything is signed.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and choose the source chain and the destination chain.
  2. Select the asset, such as a stablecoin or ETH, and enter an amount.
  3. Review the quote, the amount received and the fees.
  4. Approve the token once if it is not the native coin, then sign the bridge transaction.
  5. Wait for the transfer to arrive on the destination chain and check your wallet there.

Check the destination address and network before confirming; a transfer to the wrong place cannot be reversed.

Fees & gas

What bridging costs

Gas on the source chain

Approving and sending are transactions on the source chain, paid in that chain's native coin. See how gas works.

Bridge and messaging fees

A transfer usually carries a protocol fee and a cross-chain messaging fee. Both are folded into the quote, so the amount received is shown net of them.

Native coin on the destination

Once funds arrive you need some native coin on the destination chain to pay gas for anything you do next, such as swapping or approving.

Security

Staying safe

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. This interface never holds your funds or private keys.

Bridge-specific risks

  • Bridges hold large pools and are frequent targets, so smart-contract risk is real.
  • Double-check the destination chain and the receiving address before you sign.
  • Confirm you are sending the right token; lookalike tokens exist on every chain.
  • Revoke ERC-20 allowances you no longer need.
  • Start with a small test transfer when moving a large amount.
FAQ

Stargate FAQ

What is Stargate?

Stargate is a cross-chain bridge built on LayerZero. It moves stablecoins and ETH between supported chains using liquidity pools on each side rather than wrapped copies of the token.

What is LayerZero's role in the bridge?

LayerZero is the messaging layer that carries the transfer instruction from the source chain to the destination chain. The bridge uses that message to release the asset from the destination pool.

What do unified liquidity pools mean for me?

You deposit into a pool on the source chain and receive the asset from a pool on the destination chain. This usually gives you the asset itself, but very large transfers can be limited by pool liquidity.

How long does a transfer take?

It depends on the chains involved and their confirmation times. The interface shows the status of a transfer, and funds are not at the destination until the final transaction is confirmed there.

Do I need gas on the destination chain?

You need native coin on the destination chain to do anything with the funds once they arrive. Bridging does not pay gas for your later transactions, so plan a small balance there.

Does this interface hold my funds?

No. It is non-custodial: you connect your own wallet and sign each transaction, and assets move through on-chain contracts. There is no account to create.
